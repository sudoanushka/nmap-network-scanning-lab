Scan Type: TCP SYN Stealth Scan/ Port scanning

Tool: Nmap 7.99

Command:
nmap -p- [192.168.x.x]

Objective:
To scan all 65,535 TCP ports on the target and determine whether any ports are accessible/open.

Target Status: 
Host is Up with approximately 0.28 seconds latency.

Port Results:
No open ports were reported. All 65,535 ports were in ignored/filtered states: 33,050 filtered (no response) 
and 32,485 filtered (network unreachable).

Observation:
The first screenshot shows the SYN Stealth Scan progressing from approximately 25% to 99%. 
The second screenshot shows the completed scan and final results. The scan took 
approximately 121.80 seconds to complete.

Key Learning:
Learned how to perform a full TCP port scan using -p- and interpret Nmap results, 
particularly the difference between filtered ports and open/closed ports.
