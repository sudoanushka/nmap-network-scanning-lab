Scan Type: OS & Service detection/ Comprehensive Scan

Tool: Nmap 7.99

Command: nmap -A [192.168.x.x]

Objective:
To perform comprehensive reconnaissance by combining OS detection, service/version detection, default NSE scripts,
 and traceroute against the target.

Target Status:
Host is Up with approximately 0.079 seconds latency.

Port Results:
Nmap scanned the default 1,000 TCP ports. All 1,000 ports were reported as filtered (no-response), with 
no open or closed ports identified.

Observation:
Nmap could not obtain enough port information for reliable OS fingerprinting and displayed multiple 
possible OS/device matches, including network cameras, routers, webcams, Linux-based systems, 
and other network devices. The results were explicitly marked as potentially unreliable. 
The scan also performed traceroute using TCP port 80, showing 1 hop to the target.

Key Learning:
Learned how the -A option combines multiple Nmap capabilities, including OS detection, 
service detection, NSE-based enumeration, and traceroute, to provide a broader view of a target host.
